INTRODUCTION
Since 2009, Rwanda has used RapidSMS, an open source software solution using SMStocollect
data, to accelerate the reduction of maternal, newborn and child mortality. CHWs (communityhealth workers) use the system during their routine activities supporting maternal, neonatal andchild health (MNCH) at the community. It is used to track the pregnancy life cycle, neonatal care(NBC) and postnatal care (PNC), community case management (CCM) and nutrition status(CBN);
it tracks a mother and newborn through the first 5 years of the child’s continuumlife. Additional
scope has been developed to cover family planning, tuberculosis, and Malaria. Over the years, the needs of the end users as well as health managers have shifted. Thishasrequired an upgrade of the RapidSMS system to a more responsive systemthat; can integratewith other health data tools, such as DHIS2 and OpenMRS, have enhanced capacity for dataanalysis, is easier to support and maintain and use,, and can accommodate other health programsimplemented at the community level without trading off functionality and efficiency. In that regard,
the RapidSMS in Rwanda transitioned to Rapidpro to continue with the gains made in thehealth
3system so far. Rapid pro will solve the aforementioned challenges as well as work with thenational
RapidPro infrastructure backend to support the community health information system(CHIS). RapidPro is an Open Source platform that uses interactive messaging systems throughaneasyvisual interface
1
. It is used to send and receive SMS messages that can be used to collect dataand send SMS reminders. Data collected is then used for data analysis to give insights that areused in decision making. It is easy to use with user friendly interfaces that help the CHWs conduct
their functions. A CHW will be able to register clients and submit health events. In turn, thesystemwill send SMS reminders, feedback and notifications to CHWs when the clients services aredueto facilitate follow-up. This guide aims to provide a clear step by step guide to the end-user, on how to use andinteract
with the Rapidpro system to conduct daily tasks at the community level. The guide will usetext
as well as screenshots and other media that will help the user understand how to use thesystemquickly and clearly.
41.0 GETTING STARTED
The CHWs will be registered on the OpenMRS system. The registration will include their locationdetails and telephone contacts. The CHW’s contact information will then be pulledintotheCHIS_Rwanda group on Rapidpro. Only contacts in the group are able to interact withtheRapidPro flows. All old CHW data will be migrated into the OpenMRS system and only new contacts will needtobe added to the system. Once the contacts are added, the CHWs will be able to sendtherespective SMS forms. Do note that a CHW will need to be first registered before they cansubmit
SMS forms. End users will be provided with a short code that they will submit the SMS forms to. Responsesto the CHWs will also be sent through the same short code.
